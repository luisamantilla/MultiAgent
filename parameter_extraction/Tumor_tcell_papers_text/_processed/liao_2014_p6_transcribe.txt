It has been reported in [30] that IL27 can enhance the population  
of CD8+ T cells. Moreover, IL27 also enhances I10 produced by  
CD8+ T cells to inhibit the tumor growth, but at the same time it  
suppresses the pro-inflammatory cytokine IL6 secreted by CD8+ T  
cells. In spite of its inhibition of IL6, IL27 still promotes CD8+ T cells  
to suppress the tumor growth.  
In view of these experimental results we expect the total mass of  
I10 to increase from cases (i) to (iv), the total CD8+ T cell  
population to increase from cases (i) to (iv), and the total  
population of cancer cells to decrease from cases (i) to (iv), as  
time progresses.  
Correspondingly, we associate with the four cases (i) – (iv)  
increasing values of the parameter $\alpha_{27}$:  

$\alpha_{27} = 0$ and $\hat{I}_{27}(r,0)=0, r\in[0,2]$ in Figures. 2–5,  
in case (i) of J558–Ctrl tumor cells,  

$\alpha_{27} = 0$ and $\hat{I}_{27}(r,0)=0, r\in[0,3]$ in Figures. 6–9,  
in case (ii) of J558–Ctrl tumor cells,  

$\alpha_{27} = 324\times i.e., \alpha_{27} = 1.5\times 10^{-5} pg/(cell/day),$ in case (iii),  

$\alpha_{27} = 324\times \xi i.e., \alpha_{27} = 7.5\times 10^{-5} pg/(cell/day),$ in case (iii),  

$\alpha_{27} = 324\times 20\times i.e., \alpha_{27} = 3\times 10^{-4} pg/(cell/day),$ in case (iv).  

In Figures 2-5, we took $L=1 cm$ such that tumor cells are not  
visible near the boundary $r=1 cm$ for all time $t\in[0,90days]$.  
Figure 2 shows the time-dependent profiles of  

$I_{27}, I10, T_{r},$ and total populations of CD8+ T cells and cancer cells.  
We see that growth/decrease of these variables, as $\alpha_{27}$ varies,  
corresponds qualitatively to the experiments in [30]. Figures 3-5  
show significant spatial variations of these variables at days 3,9,  
and 15, with or without production of IL27. We also see the effect of  
IL27 on cancer cells and CD8+ T cells densities at different distances  
from the point of origin of the cancer. For example, at the origin,  
at day 3 the cancer cells density changed from $4.95\times 10^8/cell/cm^3$  
with no treatment by IL27 to $4.65\times 10^8/cell/cm^3$ with largest  
production of IL27, while at day 15 it changed from  
$4.2\times 10^8/cell/cm^3$ with no treatment to $1.7\times 10^8/cell/cm^3$ with  
largest production of IL27. Similarly, at the origin, the CD8+ T cell  
density increased at day 3 from $2.3\times 10^6/cell/cm^3$ without  
treatment to $3.3\times 10^6/cell/cm^3$ with largest production of IL27,  
whereas at day 15 the density increased even more significantly  
from $3.5\times 10^6/cell/cm^3$ with no IL27 treatment to $9\times 10^6/cell/cm^3$  
with maximal production of IL27. Note that Figure 5E and 5F show  
that the tumor cell density is almost zero near the boundary  
$r=1 cm$ and the tumor cells concentrate in the region $r=0.5 cm$.  
Tumor initiating in interstitial organs can also be treated by IL27,  
but the mechanism for introducing IL27 will depend on the location  
of the tumor. For example, in colitis induced colon cancer, one  
could use rectal wash which were programmed to express IL27 [42].  
Oncolytic virus which are engineered to produce IL27 within tumor  
cells could turn the tumor into immunogenic, thus enabling the  
immune system to reject the tumor.
