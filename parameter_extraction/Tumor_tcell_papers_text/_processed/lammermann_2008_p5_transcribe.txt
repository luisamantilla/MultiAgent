The fact that leukocytes are able to move autonomously generates 
a new level of regulatory possibilities. Because surface-bound chemo- 
kines and other immobilized extracellular signals do trigger integrin 
affinity (unlike soluble chemokines), leukocyte integrins should no 
longer be viewed as force transducers during locomotion but as 
switchable immobilizing anchors that stop, slow down or confine 
high intrinsic motility to specifically assigned surfaces30,31. The role 
of integrins is therefore mainly to mediate retention, invasion, cell– 
cell communication and cell–cell adhesion32. 

METHODS SUMMARY 
Generation of integrin-deficient leukocytes. Integrins and talin1 were targeted 
by generating mice with the genotype β1 
lox/lox (Supplementary Fig. 1), β2 
lox/lox 
(ref. 32), β7 
–/– (ref. 33), β1 
–/– (ref. 34), Mx1Cre+ (ref. 35) and talin1 
lox/lox 
(ref. 36), Mx1Cre+37, respectively. Cre expression in the haematopoietic system 
was induced by intraperitoneal injection of 250 μg poly(I:C) (Amersham 
Biosciences). 10–14 after knockout induction DCs were generated from bone 
marrow suspension and matured with lipopolysaccharide (LPS). The DCs were 
either generated from aggregates and remaining integrin-positive contaminants by 
magnetic sorting (Miltenyi Biotech). DCs used for migration assays were >99% 
enriched for β1, β2 and β7 knockout cells. 

In situ live cell imaging. For live cell microscopy, mouse ears were 
mechanically separated in dorsal and ventral halves, fluorescently stained with 
LYVE-1 antibody and immobilized with the epidermal side down. The mice 
were avoided with fluorescently labelled DCs and time lapse movies were 
recorded with an automated MZ 16 FA stereomicroscope (Leica 
Systems). 

In vitro 3D chemotaxis assays. Cells were suspended in PureCol (INAMED) and 
set in custom chambers with a gradient of recombinant CCL19 (R&D Systems, 
16 ng ml–1). After polymerization, cells were overlaid with culture medium 
containing 100 ng ml–1 CCL19. For experiments with Jurkat or K3 DG3, puri- 
fied T cells were pretreated with 10 μM cytochalasin D, 10 μM blebbistatin or 
10 μM Y27632 (Calbiochem) for 30 min at 37 °C. For experiments with human 
muscle visualization, SYTO-dyes (Invitrogen) were used for visualization of 
nuclei in thin muscle sections. 

For visualization of actin, cells were fixed and permeabilized 
using phalloidin (Texas Red, Molecular Probes) and imaged with a confocal 
microscope (Leica). 

Statistical analysis. t-tests and analysis of variance (ANOVA) were performed 
after data were confirmed to fulfil the criteria of normal distribution and equal 
variance, otherwise Kruskal–Wallis tests or Mann–Whitney U-tests were 
applied. If overall ANOVA or Kruskal–Wallis tests were significant, we per- 
formed a post hoc test. Analyses were performed with Sigma Stat 2.03. For 
further statistical details see Supplementary Table.
