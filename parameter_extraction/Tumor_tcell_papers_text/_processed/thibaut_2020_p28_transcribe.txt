    Life sciences study design

    All studies must disclose on these points even when the disclosure is negative.

    Sample size
    No sample size calculation was performed. Sample sizes were determined based on previous experiments in the laboratory, in order to get statistically significant results and limit mice number at the same time.

    Data exclusions
    For microscopy experiments, mice in which the fluorescent signal to noise ratio was too low were not analyzed. The criteria for exclusion was the inability to discriminate unambiguously individual cell contours.

    Replication
    Experiments were repeated several times, as indicated in figure legends. All repetitions of the experiments yielded similar results. Mice were housed in the same facility, in the same room and were age-matched as best as possible in every experiments and between different experiments. At the time of injection, tumor cells were less than one month in culture and all fluorescent cell lines were prepared from the same original uncloned line.

    Randomization
    Age- and sex-matched mice were used. For experiments using B16.F10 melanoma cells, different groups of mice were randomly attributed to different experimental conditions. For experiments using B cell lymphoma, different groups of mice were attributed to different experimental conditions according to the percentage of tumor cells in the blood, in order to have comparable groups in terms of tumor growth before T cells injection

    Blinding
    The experimentators were not blinded to experimental conditions during experiments of experiments analysis. Experiments and analyses were often performed by the same person.
