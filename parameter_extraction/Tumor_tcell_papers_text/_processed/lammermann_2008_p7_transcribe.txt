Ex vivo wide-field microscopy in ear dermis. Ears of killed C57BL/6 mice were 
removed and mechanically split into dorsal and ventral halves. During separa- 
tion the dermis detached from the cartilage layer that remained on one of the 
two halves. The dermal half was left unfixed and incubated with a rabbit poly- 
clonal anti-LYVE-1 antibody (R&D Systems), washed with PBS and stained with 
A488 conjugated anti-rabbit secondary antibody (Molecular Probes). The 
stained ears were mounted on custom-made migration chambers with the 
dermal surface uppermost. Fluorescent images were recorded with 
MetaMorph (Molecular Devices) as described above. For time-lapse analysis, 
labelled DCs (see above) were re-suspended in culture medium, added on top of 
the dermis and incubated for 30 min at 37 °C, 5% CO_2. After gently washing 
away non-infiltrated DCs, the ear sheets were imaged in a custom-built migra- 
tion chamber using a fully motorized upright Leica MZ 16 FA stereomicroscope 
equipped with a Spot camera and operated via MetaMorph software (Universal 
Imaging/Visitron Systems). For multiple immunofluorescence stainings 
(Supplementary Fig. 5), ear sheets were fixed with 1% PFA after 10 min incuba- 
tion of DCs and stained as described in the re-evacuation section. Sections of 
the ear dermis were stained with anti-CD11b, anti-F4/80 and anti-LYVE-1 anti- 
bodies to identify macrophages in lymph node homing. 
In vitro chemotaxis assays. In vitro chemotaxis assays were performed in 
24-well plates with 5-µm-pore-size Transwell inserts (Corning). DCs were 
labelled with TAMRA and CFSE as above and 1 x 10^5 DCs were added to the 
upper chamber. Chemokines were applied to the lower chamber. After 3 h, 
migrated cells were collected and analysed using flow cytometry. 
In vitro adhesion assays. Adhesion assays were performed in 96-well plates coated 
with 10 µg ml^-1 fibronectin (Calbiochem) or 10 µg ml^-1 recombinant mouse 
ICAM-1 (R&D). 3 x 10^5 DCs per well were seeded in RPMI medium containing 
10% FCS in the presence of 200 ng ml^-1 LPS and incubated for 30 min at 5% CO_2, 
37 °C. Small numbers of LPS-primed DCs were seeded in the presence of 
200 ng ml^-1 polyclonal anti-β1-integrin antibody (Calbiochem) for 30 min. Cells were 
washed normally 3 times with PBS, 1% BSA and subsequently photo-document- 
ed manually. Time-lapse video microscopy of chemokine-induced migra- 
tion of LPS-matured DCs (see Supplementary Video 4) was performed on 
10 µg ml^-1 mouse CCL21 (R&D)-coated plates of cultured tissue.
