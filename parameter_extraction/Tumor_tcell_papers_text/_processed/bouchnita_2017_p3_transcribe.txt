In the model, an asymmetric T cell division is considered as shown in Fig. 1. Naïve T cell entering the draining lymph node is recruited into the immune response after the contact interaction via the T cell receptor (TCR) with APC presenting the foreign antigen. The activation and prolonged contact with APC can result in asymmetry of the progeny.
