stemness (fig. S14). Ultimately, the subcellular 
location of AcCoA determines T cell stemness, 
type and function. Our finding that Acss1 was 
induced in the setting of TCF1hi is consistent 
with reports indicating that acetate becomes the 
major carbon source for metabolism during 
the starvation state, and provides the basis for

tumors. We discovered that provision of sup-

Our data support intervention and reprogram-
