Adoptive Transfer  
Naive CD8+ cells were purified (>90%) from the spleens of P14 mice  
(CD45.2+) by positively sorting with anti-CD8 magnetic beads (Milt-  
enyi Biotec) and adoptively transferred (10^6) by i.v. injection into  
C57BL/6 CD45.1 mice and after 1 day infected with LCMV. To en-  
sure P14 CD8 T cell division, recipients were given BrdU in their  
drinking water (0.8 mg/ml) for 8 days after LCMV infection (Phillips  
et al., 2004).  

In Vivo CTL Assays  
To measure ex vivo CTL activity after LCMV infection, RMA targets  
were pulsed with gp33 (10^-7 M) for 1 hr and labeled with 51Cr,  
then incubated with viable splenic leukocytes over a range of ratios  
in quadruplicate. The percentage specific lysis was determined after  
4 hr as follows: % specific release = (specific release − spontaneous  
release)/(maximum release × spontaneous release) × 100 (Oper-  
man et al., 2001). The percentage specific lysis of RMA cells in the  
absence of gp33 was <10% and the spontaneous release was  
<10% of the maximum release.  

Subcellular Fractionation of CTLs  
Splenokine (50^6/ml) from B6 P14 mice, Spi6 KO P14 mice, or Spi6  
KO × GFR KO P14 mice were cultured with LCMV gp33 peptide  
(KAVYNFATM) (10^-6 M) and IL-2 (10 U/ml) for 2 days to generate  
CTLs (Phillips et al., 2004). CTLs were lysed by sonication in hypo-  
tonic buffer (50 mM PIPES, 50 mM KCL, 5 mM EGTA, 2 mM MgCl2,  
5 mM DTT [pH 7.6]), then centrifuged at 3000 × g for 10 min to re-  
move nuclei, then 15,000 × g for 30 min to give cytosol (supernatant)  
and granule (pellet) fractions (Sun et al., 1996). The granule pellet  
was resuspended in 1% Triton X-100, 10 mM Tris-HCL, 150 mM  
NaCl (pH 7.6) for 30 min on ice.  

Antiserum  
Antiserum was raised to a Spi6 peptide (amino acids 35–47)  
(ACE-ILPDKQIRYSLYR) was raised in rabbits by standard proce-  
dures (Cocalico et al., 1995). In brief, four rabbits were immunized  
with the peptide conjugated to KLH and then boosted twice with in-  
complete Freund’s adjuvant. Anti-Spi6 antibodies were affinity  
purified on two columns of immunizing peptide, eluted with 0.1 M  
glycine, and then dialyzed against PBS. Protein (50 μg) was resolved by  
SDS-PAGE and transferred to nitrocellulose and probed with anti-Spi6  
antiserum (7 μg/ml) and goat anti-rabbit IgG conjugated to horse-  
radish peroxidase (HRP) (2 μg/ml) (Sigma-Aldrich), then visualized by  
chemiluminescence (ECL kit, Amersham). Blots were also probed  
with goat anti-mouse GrB (0.2 μg/ml) or B3/B5 systems) and anti-goat  
IgG HRP (2 μg/ml) (Sigma Aldrich) or with anti-syntenin and coatal-  
bodo clone ACNT005 (0.5 μg/ml) (Sigma Aldrich) and anti-mouse  
IgG-HRP (2 μg/ml) (Sigma-Aldrich).  

Protease Assay  
Colorimetric assays for GrB were performed in reaction buffer with  
Ac-IEPD-AFC at 0.2 mM (Liu et al., 2003) or GrA with BLT (N-ben-  
zyloxycarbonyl-L-lysine thiobenzyl ester) at 0.2 mM (Podack et al.,  
1991) and Ellman’s reagent at 1.75 mM in reaction buffer (100 mM  
Tris-HCl) at 30°C. Assays for caspase 3 were performed in reaction  
buffer with PIPES (pH 7.4), 8 mM DTT, 2 mM EDTA, 0.1% CHAPS  
at 30°C with Ac-DEVD-pNA (Calbiochem) at 0.2 mM (Liu et al., 2003).  
Protease activity was determined by normalizing to the amount of  
protein. Units of activity are was defined before at equal amounts.  
To inhibit GrB (Gong et al., 1999; Podack et al., 1991), Z-AAD  
(OEM)-CMK (12.5 μM; Sigma Aldrich) was added for the duration  
of 2 day cultures. Under these conditions, we observed 100% inhibi-  
tion of GrB in both the cytosolic and cytolytic fractions.  

C1M  
P14 CTLs were seeded on wells of poly-L-lysine–coated slides, then  
fixed and permeabilized in acetone/methanol and blocked with 10%  
normal horse serum in PBS for 60 min at 20°C. P14 CTLs were  
stained with anti-murine GrB Ab (1/800; R&D Systems) and anti-mu-  
rine P1-8 mAb (1:100; Kamiya Biomedecal Inc) for 30 min at 20°C  
in 2% BSA/PBS, then biotin-conjugated mouse anti-rat mAb (1:100;  
BD Pharmingen) and FITC conjugated rabbit anti-rat antibody  
(1:200; Molecular Probes) for 30 min at 20°C. After washing with  
PBS, cells were further incubated with streptavidin-PE (1 μg/ml)  

BD Pharmingen) and DAPI (Sigma Aldrich), washed, and mounted  
in Vectashield (Vector Labs). Cells were examined with a Leica  
SP2 AOBS spectral laser scanning confocal microscope operated  
with the software LCS 2.5v1347. The mean number of granules  
was determined from the counting of multiple cell images (10 cells  
in 3 separate counts, n = 300) with a granule defined as point of stain-  
ing with a diameter >0.1 μm.  

TEM  
Cells were stained with uranyl acetate/lead citrate, as described in  
Supplemental Experimental Procedures. An electron microscope  
(FEI/Technai F30) at 300 kV was used for examination. Electron-den-  
sco vesicles were counted in 64 μm^2 cell areas (n = 300), and then  
the total mean number per cell was determined assuming a mean  
cell volume of 523 μm^3 (mean number per 64 μm^2 × 8.2 = mean total  
number per cell).  

Statistics  
The significance of difference was measured using two-tailed Stu-  
dent’s t tests.
