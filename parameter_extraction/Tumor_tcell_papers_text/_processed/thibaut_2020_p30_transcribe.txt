Instrument
A majority of the data was acquired using a Cytoflex LX (serial number B4A2021). Some data were acquired using a BD Canto II

Software
BD FACS Diva was used for BD Canto II-acquired Data. For cytoflex-acquired data, CytExpert version 2.3 was used

Cell population abundance
No cell sorting was performed during experiments

Gating strategy
For experiments using B16.F10 melanoma cells, cell doublets were gated out based on SSC-A/SSC-H followed by an additional 
gating based on FSC-A/FSC-W. Live cells were isolated by FSC-/SSC-A gating. Injected OT-I T cells were distinguished based on 
their GFP expression. B16.F10 cells expressing OVA or not were then gated on based on their expression of TdTomato and 
membrane CFP respectively. A back-gating on FSC-A/SSC-A was used to confirm the discrimination of B16.F10 cells.

For experiments using Eμ-myc B cell lymphoma in vivo, cell doublets were excluded as described above. CFP and GFP were used 
to detect Eμ-myc expressing OVA or not respectively. CD8 staining enabled further distinction of GFP+ OT-I T cells from GFP+ 
tumor cells. 
In a distinct panel, CD45 enabled distinction of hematopoietic cells: OVA- Eμ-myc cells and OT-I T cells were gated out using GFP 
and CD8 expression as above. Likewise, OVA+ Eμ-myc cells were gated out by their CFP expression. Among remaining cells, 
myeloid cells were distinguished by their CD11b expression and further divided into monocytes (Ly6C high Ly6G-) and 
neutrophils (Ly6C+ Ly6G+). Finally, NK cells were identified as CD45+ CD11b+ NK1.1+. A figure describing the gating strategy that 
was used to identify tumor, NK, OT-I T cells, as well as monocytes and neutrophils, is provided in the manuscript.
