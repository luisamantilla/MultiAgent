Transfection. Human melanoma 624mel cells were transfected with the
pcDNA3 plasmid (Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA) encoding human B7-H1 gene^4 or
H-2K^d/2m and the empty vector pcDNA3 (Mock/624mel), and were se-
lected on the basis of G418 resistance. Mock/P815, B7-1/P815, B7-H1/P815,
and B7-1/B7-H1/P815 tumors were transfected with the corresponding plas-
mids as described previously^5, and the expression of surface molecules was
confirmed by FACS analysis using specific monoclonal antibodies.

Generation of antigen-specific CTLs. Tumor antigen–specific CD8+
human T-cell lines were generated from peripheral blood mononuclear
cells from a healthy HLA-A2–positive donor by in vitro stimulation using
624mel cells loaded with corresponding peptide epitopes, as described^5.
M15 is a human CTL line that specifically recognizes an HLA-A2–restricted
epitope (IMDQVPFSV) of the gp100 antigen. M9 is an HLA-B7–restricted
CTL clone that recognizes an epitope (RPHVQYLCI) of carcinoembryonic
antigen. CTL activity was determined by a 4-h ^51Cr-release cytotoxicity
assay. To activate 2C T cells, 4 × 10^7 CD8+ T cells were purified from spleen
and lymph nodes using MACS beads (Miltenyi Biotec, Auburn, California)
and subsequently stimulated with irradiated BALB/c splenocytes and
10 IU/ml human IL-2 for 3 d. Viable 2C T cells were purified with
Lympholyte-M (Cedarlane Laboratories, Hornby, Ontario, Canada).

Cell apoptosis assays. Human CD8+ (CTLs or M9) and CD4+ (M15) T
cells per well were cultured with irradiated tumor cells at 1 × 10^5 × 10^5
cells per well for 4 d. Monoclonal antibodies, fusion proteins and control
Ig were included from the start of culture. Cells were harvested at the indi-
cated times and stained with annexin V and monoclonal antibodies against
CD8. Apoptosis was calculated as the percentage of annexin V+ cells gated
in the CD8+ fractions. To assay for apoptosis of 2C T cells, female RAG-1^–/–
mice were inoculated i.p. with 1 × 10^7 Mock/P815 or B7-1/B7-H1/P815
cells. Three days later, pre-activated 2C T cells (2.5 × 10^7) were injected i.p. into
each mouse. Peritoneal cells were collected, counted at each time point as
indicated and stained with 1B2 monoclonal antibody and annexin V.
Apoptosis was calculated as the percentage of annexin V+ cells gated in the
1B2+ fraction. Data are represented as means ± s.d. of cells from 3 mice for
each point in at least 3 experiments.

To induce apoptosis in polyclonal CT cells, purified human peripheral
blood CD8+ (95% CD3+) at 2 × 10^7 per well were preeducated with tumor
plates precoated with 0.5 µg/ml of monoclonal antibody against 2C3
(PharMingen, San Diego, California) and 10 µg/ml of either immobilized B7-H1Ig
or control Ig (mouse IgG2a) for 72 h. T cells at 1 × 10^7 per sample were
stained with annexin V (5 µg per test) and propidium iodide (PI; 5 µg/ml;
Sigma) and the samples were analyzed by FACS. Apoptosis was calcu-
lated as the percentage of annexin V+ PI cells in the viable fraction.
For blockade of apoptosis, the human PD-1Ig (30 µg/ml) and the neutral-
izing monoclonal antibody against human IL-10 (5 µg/ml, JES3-9D7,
PharMingen) or against human FasL (8 µg/ml, NOK-1, PharMingen) were
added from the onset.

Mouse studies. Female DBA/2 (H-2^d) and RAG-1^– mice (H-2^d) were pur-
chased from the Jackson Laboratory (Bar Harbor, Maine). Mice (2–6) mice
were used per study. F. Carbone. RAG-1^– mice (H-2^d) mice were inoculated
with 1 × 10^7 mock/P815 or B7-H1/P815 cells i.p. After 3 d, 150 µg per mouse
of control antibody (hamster IgG) or monoclonal antibody against mouse
B7-H1 was injected i.p. 2 h before the transfer of activated 2C T cells. One
