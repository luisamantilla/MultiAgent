2.3. Fluorescent labeling of alpha casein

Alpha casein (Sigma, St. Louis, MO) was labeled with
BODIPY FL (Molecular Probes, Eugene, OR). A 5 mg/ml
solution of AC was prepared by dissolving 75 mg of AC in
14 ml of doubly distilled water ddH2O), adjusting the pH
to 8.3 with 0.1 M NaOH and adjusting the volume to 15 ml
with ddH2O. The pH of the AC solution was adjusted to pH
8.3 because the reaction is most efficient between pH 7.5 and
8.3 [16] and addition of BODIPY FL will slightly lower the
pH. For labeling, 1 ml of the AC solution was combined with
68 µl of 5 mg/ml BODIPY FL and mixed using a vortex mixer
for 1 h at room temperature. The labeled AC was separated
from unreacted dye by size exclusion chromatography using
Sephadex G-50 fine (Sigma, St. Louis, MO) equilibrated with
ddH2O. The BODIPY FL labeled AC eluted from the column
first and was collected. The concentration of the labeled AC
was determined by the BCA assay (Pierce, Rockford, IL) and
was approximately 8 mg BODIPY FL labeled AC/ml.

2.4. Dendritic cell culture

Dendritic cells were derived from the bone marrow of
10–12 weeks old female BALB/c mice. The mice were anes-
thetized with 90 mg ketamine and 10 mg xylazine/kg and then
euthanized. The femurs and tibias were collected in ice-cold
Hank’s balanced salt solution (HBSS, Sigma, St. Louis, MO)
with 50 µg/ml gentamycin. The muscles were removed with
a scalpel and by rubbing the bones with tissue. The ends
of the bones were then cut off with scissors and crushed.
The bone marrow was flushed out with 2–3 ml of media in
a syringe with a 25-gauge needle. All bone marrow cells
were collected and washed twice with HBSS. The bone mar-
row cells were cultured in 10 cm petri dishes containing ap-
proximately 5 × 105 cells/ml in 10 ml total volume. The cells
were maintained at 37 ◦C with 5% CO2 in RPMI 1640 with
5% fetal calf serum (Sigma, St. Louis, MO), 0.05 µM β-
mercaptoethanol (Sigma, St. Louis, MO), 3% supernatant of
J558L cells transfected with granulocyte macrophage colony
stimulating factor (GM-CSF), 2 mM l-glutamine, 100 U/ml
penicillin, 100 µg/ml streptomycin and 0.25 µg/ml ampho-
tericin B. After 3 days the nonadherent cells were collected
and discarded and 10 ml of fresh media was added. On day
5, 5 ml of media was replaced with fresh media. On day 7,
an additional 5 ml of media was added to the cells. On day 8,
the cells were collected, washed and resuspended in the me-
dia without GM-CSF. The culture contained approximately
75% immature DCs. The study was approved by the Purdue
University Animal Care and Use Committee.

2.5. Model vaccines

A 0.1 mg/ml solution of BODIPY FL labeled AC was pre-
pared by diluting an appropriate volume of BODIPY FL la-
beled AC as prepared in Section 2.3 to 10 ml with ddH2O
and adjusting the pH to 7.4. A 1 mg Al/ml suspension of each
adjuvant was prepared as described in Section 2.1. The BOD-
IPY FL labeled AC adsorbed to each adjuvant was prepared
by combining 2.5 ml of 0.1 mg BODIPY FL labeled AC/ml
with 2.5 ml of a 1 mg Al/ml suspension of each adjuvant and
mixing with a magnetic stirrer for 1 h.

2.6. Treatment of DCs with AC

Immature DCs (1 × 106 cells/ml) were treated with a me-
dia alone, solution of BODIPY FL labeled AC (0.01 mg/ml)
or a suspension of BODIPY FL labeled AC (0.01 mg/ml) ad-
sorbed to each adjuvant (0.1 mg Al/ml). Treatment groups
were set up in 6 well plates with a total volume of 3 ml/well.
For the cells treated with media alone, 0.6 ml of media was
added to 2.4 ml of DCs. For the DCs treated with a solu-
tion of AC, 0.3 ml of the 0.1 mg/ml BODIPY FL AC solution
and 0.3 ml of media were added to 2.4 ml of DCs. For DCs
treated with AC adsorbed to each adjuvant, 0.6 ml of each
model vaccine was added to 2.4 ml of DCs. Following in-
cubation at 37 ◦C for 1, 2, or 3 h, the DCs were washed
twice with 0.25 ml PBS and analyzed by confocal micros-
copy and flow cytometry.

2.7. Confocal microscopy

For analysis by confocal microscopy, 1 ml of treated DCs
was washed twice in flow buffer (1 g BSA, 1 g sodium azide
in 1 l phosphate buffered saline). After washing, 0.3 ml of 2%
paraformaldehyde was added to each sample. The cells were
stained with AlexaFluor 568 conjugated phalloidin (Molec-
ular Probes, Eugene, OR). The phalloidin solution (0.1 ml)
was diluted with 0.19 ml of PBS. The DCs were centrifuged at
1500 rpm (537 × g) for 4 min at 4 ◦C and the supernatant was
removed. The DCs were then resuspended in 0.2 ml of diluted
phalloidin and incubated at 4 ◦C for 20 min. Following incu-
bation the DCs were washed twice with 0.25 ml PBS. Each
sample was then observed by confocal microscopy at 60×
magnification. The z-series images obtained were created
with Confocal Assistant (Universal Imaging Corp., Down-
ingtown, PA) and Adobe Photoshop 7.0® (Adobe, San Jose,
CA).
