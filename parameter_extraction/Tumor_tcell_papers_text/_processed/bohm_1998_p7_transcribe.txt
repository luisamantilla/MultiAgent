FIGURE 5. Specific CTL-mediated lysis of transfected B16.F10 cells. CTLL specific for HBsAg (A, C, E) or T Ag (B, D, F) were generated in C57BL/6 mice by DNA vaccination. In a 4-h ^51Cr release assay, these CTLL specifically lysed IFN-γ-pretreated, but not nonpretreated, transfected B16.F10 targets (C, D). In a 12-h assay, these CTLL showed specific cytolytic reactivity toward noninduced B16.F10 transfectants (E, F). E:T ratios are 20:1, 10:1, 5:1, and 2.5:1.
