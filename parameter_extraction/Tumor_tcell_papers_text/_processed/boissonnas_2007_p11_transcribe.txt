The single-lens two-photon laser-scanning microscopy (TPLSM) set-ups

SHG-GFP-Rhodamine fluorescence (Fig. 3) were separated using Meta 
detector and were pixel-wise linear unmixed taken experimental fluores- 
cence spectra of nonlabeled samples as an external reference. Otherwise, 
three fluorescence channels were separated using 495 and 590 nm dichroic 
mirrors in combination with either 457/50 (SHG) or 472/30 (GFP), 520/35 
(GFP), and 624/40 nm bandpass filters. The excitation wavelength was 
900 nm. For analysis of cell migration, every 30 s during 30–60 min, four 
consecutive 360 μm2 images, with 10 μm z-spacing using the 20x objec- 
tive two-photon set-up, were artificially unfolded to obtain a real visualiza- 
tion over 1.5 μm. Images were average-projected using Image J (National 
Institutes of Health). Manual tracking of individual cells was performed 
using Metamorph software. Cells that could not be tracked for more than 
5 min were not considered to minimize projection errors inherent to the two- 
dimensional tracks analysis. The correlation analysis protocol for all 
experimental conditions to be compared were identical. The arrest coefficient 
was defined as the proportion of time each cell instantaneously calculated 
for every 30-s interval is <2 μm/min. The confinement ratio corre- 
sponds to the ratio of the distance between initial and the final positions 
of each cell to the total distance covered by the same cell. The elongation 
index is defined by the ratio between the length of long axis of the cell and 
the length of the orthogonal axis of the cell. Length and breadth were 
manually calculated in each image.
