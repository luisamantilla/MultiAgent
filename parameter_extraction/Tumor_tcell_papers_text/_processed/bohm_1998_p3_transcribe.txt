898                                                                 IMMUNE REJECTION MECHANISMS OF MURINE B16 MELANOMAS

B16.F0 and B16.F1 from the American Type Culture Collection (ATCC, 
Manassas, VA; CRL-6322, CRL-6323) and B16.F10 (from Dr. P. Anton-
sson, Lund, Sweden) were used. In most experiments, we used the 
B16.F10 line.

The bovine papilloma virus-based vector BMGneo (24) (a generous gift 
from Drs. Y. Karasuyama and F. Melchers, Basel, Switzerland) was used 
to construct the BMG/HBs expression plasmid (22) and the BMG/T-Ag-I 
expression plasmid (21). BMG/HBs and BMG/T-Ag-I vector DNA, or 
BMGneo vector DNA (without insert), were transfected into B16 cells 
using the CaPO4 method. Expression of the viral proteins was tested in B16 
transfectants labeled with 400 μCi of [35S]methionine (cat. no. SJ1015; 
Amersham, Braunschweig, Germany) in methionine-free RPMI 1640 me-
dium. Labeled cells were washed twice in PBS and extracted with lysis 
buffer (120 mM NaCl, 1% aprotinin (Trasylol, cat. no. 48764; Bayer, Le-
verkusen, Germany), 50 μM leupeptine, 0.5% Nonidet P-40, 10% glycerol, 
50 mM Tris/HCl, pH 8.0) for 30 min at 4°C. Extracts cleared by 
centrifugation (30 min, 20,000 x g, 4°C) were subsequently incubated for 
2 h at 4°C with protein-A Sepharose (cat. no. 17-0780-01; Pharmacia, 
Freiburg, Germany). The HBsAg proteins were precipitated with a poly-
clonal rabbit-anti-HBs antiserum and protein A-Sepharose, and the T-Ag-
proteins were precipitated with mAb PAb101 (25) and protein A-Sepha-
rose at 4°C. The precipitates were washed extensively in low salt (1% Tri-
tonX-100, 0.4% I P, 1 mM Tris/HCl (pH 9.0)). After two additional washes 
with 0.5% Nonidet P-40 in PBS and once again in 0.1% PBS, they were re-
covered from the A-Sepharose in 400 μl of elution buffer (1.5% SDS, 
5% β-ME, and 7 mM Tris/HCl, pH 6.8). Following an incubation for 30 
min at 37°C, SDS-PAGE samples were lyophilized and dissolved in 30 
μl of sample buffer containing glycerol and bromophenol blue. 
Immunoprecipitated samples were analyzed by SDS-PAGE.

IFN-γ treatment of B16 cells

B16 cells were cultured for 48 to 72 h in serum-free medium containing 20 
to 400 U/ml IFN-γ (rat-γ, cat. no. 1279605, Boehringer Mannheim, 
Mannheim, Germany). Cells were collected, washed, then used in flow 
cytometry, 51Cr-release or cytotoxic assays.

FCM analyses

For FCM studies, cells were suspended in PBS/0.5% (w/v) BSA supple-
mented with 0.1% (w/v) sodium azide. Nonspecific binding of Abs to Fc 
receptors was avoided by incubating cells with the mAb 2.4G2 (cat. no. 
01241D) directed against FcγRII/III (CD16/CD32) (9 μg mAb/10^6 cells/
ml). Cells were incubated with 0.5 μg/10^6 cells of the relevant Ab for 
20 min at 4°C and washed twice. In most experiments, cells were subse-
quently incubated with a second-step reagent for 20 min at 4°C. Three-
color FCM analyses were performed on a FACScan (Becton Dickinson, 
Mountain View, CA). The forward angle light scatter channel was used as 
an additional parameter to facilitate exclusion of dead cells and aggregated 
cell clumps. The following reagents and Abs from Pharmingen (Hamburg, 
Germany) were used: FITC-conjugated or biotinylated anti-CD4 
(L3T4) mAb H129.19 (cat. no. 09040D, 08012D), FITC-conjugated anti-CD4 
(L3T4) mAb RM4-5 (cat. no. 01062D), anti-CD8α (Ly-2) mAb 53-6.7 (cat. no. 
01042D), FITC-conjugated anti-CD8α mAb 53-6.7 (cat. no. 01242D), FITC-
conjugated anti-CD4 (Pgk-1) mAb RM4-7 (cat. no. 01242D), phycoerythrin 
(PE)-conjugated anti-CD80 (B7.1) mAb 16-10A1 (cat. no. 09065D), biotin-
ylated anti-CD86 mAb GL1 (cat. no. 09270D), biotinylated anti-CD95 
(Fas) mAb Jo2 (cat. no. 15042D), FITC-conjugated anti-H-2K^b mAb AF6-
88.5 (cat. no. 01040D), biotinylated anti-H-2D^b mAb 28-14-8 (cat. no. 
06323D), FITC-conjugated anti-I^A^b mAb 25-9-17 (cat. no. 08034D), PE-
conjugated anti-I^A^b mAb M5/114.15.2 (cat. no. 09175D), biotinylated anti-
CD40 mAb 3/23 (cat. no. 09062D), biotinylated anti-CD40L (gp39) mAb 
MR1 (cat. no. 09202D), FITC-conjugated Annexin V (cat. no. 33120D), Annexin 
V-PE-conjugated (cat. no. 65752X), Annexin V-FITC-conjugated (cat. no. 
65734X), and PE-, chromo- and FITC-conjugated streptavidin (cat. no. 
13052D, 13042D). The anti-CD95L (FasL) mAb N-20 (cat. no. sc-8349 
was obtained from Santa Cruz Biotechnology (Heidelberg, Germany).

Reverse transcription-PCR

Total RNA was isolated from nontransfected or transfected B16 cells (Qia-
gen minikit, cat. no. 74123) and reverse transcribed to cDNA using a 
cDNA synthesis kit (Stratagene, Frankfurt, Germany cat. no. 200402). Ali-
quots from each cDNA preparation were PCR-amplified using the forward/
reverse primers and the conditions previously described (27), and the buffer 
and polymerase from Stratagene (cat. no. 600153-81, 600153-82). The 
0.54-kb fragment was resolved on a 2% agarose gel.
