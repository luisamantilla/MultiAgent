Fig. 3 | Kinetics of IFN-γ sensing by bystander tumor cells. a, Illustration of imaging window setup and experimental timeline. At the indicated
timepoints in the 0 h to 120 h time window, intravital imaging of tumor lesions was performed. b, Sequential, intravital, two-photon imaging of
tumors consisting of 10% GFP+Ag+ tumor cells (green) and 90% CFP+ IGS reporter bystander tumor cells (cyan) was initiated 14 d after injection of
tumor cells (8 × 105 cells total) into the mouse mammary gland. On IGS reporter activation, cells gain Katushka signal (white in upper panel, red in
lower panel). A representative imaging series of a tumor region with Katushka reporter expression (top panel) and merger of GFP+Ag+ (green),
CFP+Ag+ (cyan) and Katushka reporter (red) (lower panel) signal is depicted over time. Data are representative of c. c, Quantification of the percentage
of IGS Katushka+ reporter cells in the Ag+ cell population showing the mean ± s.d. over time. Symbols represent data from different animals
(n = 5 mice for time, t = 0, 16, 24 and 32 h; n = 4 mice for t = 40, 48 and 72 h; and n = 3 mice for t = 120 h, obtained in all independent experiments).
