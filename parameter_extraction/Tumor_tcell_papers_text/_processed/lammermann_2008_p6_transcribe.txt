METHODS

Generation of integrin αvfl/fl mice. To generate a conditional floxed αv allele, a
targeting vector spanning 8.2 kb of the murine αv integrin locus27 was con-
structed. First, the 2.2 kb genomic subclone pXR2, encoding the 5' upstream
region, exon 1 and part of the intron 1 of the αv gene was used to introduce two
loxP-sites. The 5'-loxP-site including an EcoRI-restriction site to facilitate
the identification of the targeted locus was cloned into the HindIII site in the
5'-upstream region (nucleotide position, nt-pos., -310) of the αv gene. The
3'-loxP-site was introduced into the BamHI-site in intron 1 (nt-pos., +528).
The genomic subclone pMCS harbouring this BamHI-site at its 5'-end and
~6 kb 3'- downstream intron 1 sequences was used to clone a pgk-neomycin-
tandem gene selection-cassette (neoTK) flanked by two frt-sites (provided by
G. R. Martin) into the BamHI-site. These two modified genomic regions of the
αv gene were then fused at the corresponding BamHI-site to obtain the αv gene
targeting vector. ES cell transfection, selection and screening was carried out as
described27 except that R1 ES cells28 were used. Homologously targeted clones
were identified by Southern blotting of EcoRI- or BamHI-digested DNA with 5'-
or 3'-external probes A or B, respectively. To remove the neoTK selection cassette,
targeted ES cells (neoTK) were transiently transfected with the CAGGS-FLPe-
expression construct29 (provided by A. F. Stewart), and clones identified with
loss of only the selection cassette at the two flanked allele sites were chosen for
further rounds of blotting. ES cell clones were injected into blastocysts of
C57Bl/6 mice, and the resulting germline transmitting male chimaera were mated
to C57Bl/6 females to generate heterozygous αvfl/+ mice and wild-type
αv+/+ following previous standard26. Two independent lines carrying the
αvfl/fl allele were established and analysed for Mendelian inheritance in a
mixed background, αv+/+ and αvfl/fl mice show no obvious phenotype.
Southern blot and PCR analyses: wild-type, fl and mutant αv alleles were assessed
by Southern blot analysis of BamHI and/or PCR from DNA isolated from ES cells,
mouse tail biopsies or from yolk sacs of embryos as described27. In brief, 10 μg of
genomic DNA was digested with BamHI or EcoRI and probed with a 5' (αv-
labelled 5'-external probe A (αv-1.9Pst-EcoRI/XbaI DNA- fragment) or 3'-
external probe B (690-bp-BglII/SacI DNA-fragment) as described. PCR
analysis was performed with the following primers: αv-1 (5'-GCTGAGTGGG-
GAGGATTGCTG-3') and αv-2 (5'-GCTGAGTGGGGAGGATTGCTG-3') to
detect the wild-type (WT) and floxed (fl) alleles, and αv-3 (5'-GCTGAGTGGG-
GAGGATTGCTG-3') and αv-4 (5'-GCTGAGTGGGGAGGATTGCTG-3') to
detect the mutant (Δ) allele. PCR amplification products were separated through
a 1.5% agarose gel and detected by ethidium bromide staining. The positions of
the primers are located in close vicinity to the loxP-sites. PCR amplification was
performed in a 50 μl reaction mixture containing 1 mM MgCl2, 5 mM dNTPs,
buffer adjusted to a final concentration of 1 mM MgCl2, and water as standard
conditions. PCR samples were initially denatured at 94 °C for 3 min, followed by
35 cycles of 94 °C for 30 s, 60 °C for 2 min, 72 °C for 3 min, followed by
a final 10 min extension at 72 °C followed. PCR amplification products were
separated through agarose gel electrophoresis, the allele-specific primer
products, and primer orientation (forward, Fw; reverse, Rv), the PCR product
and their corresponding PCR-product sizes are as follows: Primer set (F30: 5'-
GCGGAGCACAGGAGGAACTT-3', Rv: 5'-CCTTGAGCTGAGCCTGAGTAGC-
3') (WT, 125 bp; fl, 225 bp; Δ, 325 bp). Products (F20/12: 5'-CTGGATCTGAGTGG-
GAGGATTGCTG-3', Rv: 5'-CCTTGAGCTGAGCCTGAGTAGC-3') (WT, 201/2:
614 bp) (floxed: Δ, 3', F30/12: 339 bp (pre-mutated, knockout allele,
αvΔex).

Mouse strains. Mice with constitutive deletions of integrin β7, integrin β3, CCR7
and conditional β7, flox/flox mice were described30,31. All mice were kept on a
mixed 129SV/C57BL/6 genetic background. The integrin αv conditional strains were
inter-crossed with Mx1Cre transgenic mice to generate αvΔex, β7Δex,
β7−/−, β3−/−, Mx1Cre− animals. Talinfl/flMx1Cre+ mice were crossed with
Mx1Cre transgenic mice to obtain talinΔexMx1Cre+ mice. All control
animals were on a mixed 129SV/C57BL/6 genetic background. The mice were
housed in a conventional animal facility at the Max Planck Institute of
Biochemistry, and the handling and care of the animals were in accordance with
the institutional guidelines.

Induction of the haematopoietic knockout in αvfl/fl mice. At age between 8 and 12 weeks,
mice of the desired genotype received a single intraperitoneal injection of 250 μg
Poly (I:C) (Polyinosinic-polycytidylic acid, diluted in 0.15 m phosphate-buffered
saline (PBS); Mice were killed 10–14 d after injection and knockout efficiency of
haematopoietic lineages was assessed from by flow cytometric analysis of
peripheral blood platelets26. Bone marrow (BM) of mice with ~85% integrin
β1-negative platelets were processed for dendritic cell (DC) isolation and differen-
tiation. Knockout efficiencies usually correspond well with knockout efficien-
cies of DCs and granulocytes, whereas peripheral B cells frequently show reduced
knockout efficiencies.

BM generation, separation and purification. DCs were generated from flushed
BM suspension as described previously32. At day 8–10 of culture, 250 ng ml-1
LPS (Sigma-Aldrich; E. coli0127:B8) was added overnight and the pure integrin-
deleted DCs were subsequently depleted of remaining Gr-1+ and integrin β7+
contaminants. For depletion, cells were incubated with biotinylated antibodies
against Gr-1 (RB6-8C5) and integrin β7 (Ha2/5) (both BD Pharmingen), fol-
lowed by streptavidin-mediated separation according to the manufacturers
protocol (Miltenyi biotech). The negatively selected DCs used for remaining
assays were >99% enriched for β7 integrin knockout cells and β7-deficient cells
were always further collected for integrin αv. After enrichment the cells were
used as integrin− DCs. Talin−/− DCs were generated identically and depleted for
Gr-1+ contaminants. To determine knockout efficiencies, DC lysates were
loaded on 12–15% gradient gels for electrophoresis under reduced conditions
and western blotting. Talin was detected with mouse anti-pan-talin antibody
(T3287, Sigma), and the loading control was performed with rabbit anti-mouse
actin antibody (A2066, Sigma). Integrin β7 was detected with the rabbit poly-
clonal anti-integrin β7 antibody 1244P. Integrin αv was detected with a rabbit
polyclonal anti-integrin αv antibody (Chemicon). Dual primary antibodies
were detected with anti-mouse or anti-rabbit horse radish peroxidase (HRP)
or anti-rabbit HRP (both Bio-Rad) as secondary antibodies, respectively. DCs
were collected for talin and αv for these knockout experiments, and knockout
cells were detected by talin and that talin was neither expressed nor induced as a
compensation mechanism in Mx1Cre transgenic mice subjected by staining whole
bone marrow suspension with anti-Gr-1 PE or anti-Gr-1 PE, anti-Gr-1 FITC, and
subsequently fluorescent activated cell sorting into ACSAFA1 (BD Biosciences)
or F4/80+ subsets. Sorted cells incorporating ACSAFA1 (BD Biosciences) were
treated for 30 min with 20 ng ml−1 TNF-α (Roche) at 37 °C. B lymphoblasts were
generated by mixing splenocytes through a 70 μm nylon cell strainer (BD
Biosciences) from lymphocytes with ACK lysis buffer (0.15 M NH4Cl, 1 mM
KHCO3, 0.1 mM EDTA), and subsequent magnetic cell sorting (MACS)
through two MACS MicroBeads LS columns (Miltenyi). The negatively sorted
B cells were stimulated with 10 μg ml−1 LPS (Sigma) for 72 h at 37 °C, 5% CO2.
Streptavidin-mediated separation of integrin β7 cells (see DCs) resulted in
