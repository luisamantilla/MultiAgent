\begin{align*}
\frac{\partial \hat{I}_{27}}{\partial \hat{t}} &= D_{I27} \frac{1}{\hat{r}^2} \frac{\partial}{\partial \hat{r}} \left( \hat{r}^2 \frac{\partial \hat{I}_{27}}{\partial \hat{r}} \right) + \hat{a} \hat{c} \hat{I}_{27} - \hat{\mu}_{27} \hat{I}_{27} \\
\frac{\partial \hat{I}_{10}}{\partial \hat{t}} &= D_{I10} \frac{1}{\hat{r}^2} \frac{\partial}{\partial \hat{r}} \left( \hat{r}^2 \frac{\partial \hat{I}_{10}}{\partial \hat{r}} \right) + \hat{\sigma}_{I10} \frac{\hat{I}_{27}}{\hat{I}_{27} + \hat{I}_{10}} \\

Numerical simulation

The model (6) was simulated, in nondimensional variables, using matlab with d\hat{r} = 0.02 and d\hat{t} = 0.01 (i.e., dr = 0.01 cm and dt = 1/3 day in dimensional units). Four cases were considered:

(i) J558-Ctrl tumor cells.

(ii) J558-IL-27 tumor cells with small production rate of IL-27.

(iii) J558-IL-27 tumor cells with moderate production rate of IL-27.

(iv) J558-IL-27 tumor cells with large production rate of IL-27.
